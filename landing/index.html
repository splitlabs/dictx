From your voice to the text field in four steps

  1. Hold the shortcut

    Press and hold Option and Space. The menu bar icon switches to recording.

  2. Talk normally

    Dictx trims the silence while you speak, so pauses cost nothing.

  3. Let go

    The model you picked transcribes the recording on your Mac.

  4. Keep typing

    The text is pasted into the field you were in. Prefer tapping to holding? Switch to toggle mode in Settings.

What it does on a Mac

Your audio stays here
Recording and transcription run on your Mac. Dictx goes online to download a model and, with Pro, to check for updates.
Two permissions, and why
Microphone, so it can hear you. Accessibility, so it can paste the text into the app you were using.
Models for your hardware
Whisper Small, Medium, Turbo, and Large use the GPU. Parakeet V3 is tuned for the CPU. Switch between them in Settings.
Cleanup, if you want it
Option, Shift, and Space runs the transcript through a post-processing prompt. Use a provider you set up, or Apple Intelligence on Apple silicon with macOS Tahoe 26.
Notes into Obsidian
Save transcripts as Markdown notes with YAML frontmatter, or append them to your daily note.
Scriptable
Start or stop recording from a script: /Applications/Dictx.app/Contents/MacOS/Dictx --toggle-transcription

Questions

Does my audio leave the Mac?

No. Recording and transcription happen locally. Post-processing is off until you turn it on, and then it uses the provider you chose.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple silicon and Intel Macs each have their own download. Apple Intelligence cleanup needs Apple silicon and macOS Tahoe 26.

Why does it ask for Accessibility access?

macOS treats pasting into another app as controlling it. Dictx needs that permission to put the transcript where your cursor is.

How do I activate Pro?

After checkout you get a license key that starts with dxp-. Paste it in Settings, under About, then Activate Dictx Pro.

Is Dictx open source?

Yes, under GPL-3.0. Read the code on GitHub, build it yourself, or send a pull request.

Is there a Windows or Linux version?

Yes. Installers for both are on the GitHub releases page.
